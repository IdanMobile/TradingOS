## What “verified strategy” means here

A verified strategy is one exact:

`StrategyVersion × market × instrument × timeframe × configuration × environment`

It is not a popular family name, a positive backtest, a platform leaderboard result,
or a bot that happens to run. It requires:

1. exact source, author/version, license, and ambiguity records;
2. a complete canonical strategy specification and immutable hash;
3. point-in-time, checksum-pinned data and complete provenance;
4. a preregistered family, hierarchy, trial budget, costs, splits, metrics, and stop rules;
5. causal signal/fill semantics and independent known-answer fixtures;
6. retained all-trial evidence with no hidden search stages;
7. realistic after-cost economics, OOS/walk-forward, perturbation, regime, benchmark,
   capacity/liquidity, tail, and multiple-testing review;
8. cross-engine or independent reference reproduction;
9. independent risk and supervisor review;
10. `COMPLETE_APPROVABLE` and `promotion_eligible=true` for that exact context.

A strategy can still lose after passing research. “Verified” means the evidence is
decision-useful and the failure modes are controlled; it never means guaranteed profit.

## What “bot running” means

The first lawful running-bot milestone is the locked local
`SYNTHETIC_LOCAL_SIMULATOR`, with synthetic capital and no venue credentials. It may
activate only after a matching strategy is validation-approved and the operator grants
HG-3.

A venue testnet/demo bot is a later, separate qualification stage requiring HG-4,
current venue/account eligibility, security review, a typed adapter, and reconciliation
evidence. A live-capital bot requires S3 exit and non-delegable HG-5. The supervisor
never holds credentials, changes a gate, or sends an order.
